What is QntaAI?

QntaAI is a AI-Search engine made by QntaTechnologies and a team, consisting three members. Inspired by perplexity, an AI anawer engine, with search modes— 1. Quick - Fast answers with LLM's own language as per they are trained on. 2. Search - for live quires like news and live data fetched by direct internet. 3. Research - for multiple quires and user's deep understanding releted with live info like live research papers across the world. Model selection— there are three models helps user choose their own experience. 1. GPT 2. Gemini 3. Grok
